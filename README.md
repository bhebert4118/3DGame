# Current Target
The player is inside of a guard's room, watching the jail cell through cctv footage. The goal is to complete various puzzles to help the prisoner escape.


# Team Roles
Will - Environment and Level Design
Responsible for building the main prison environment, including rooms, hallways, props, lighting, collision, spawn points, guard waypoints, and puzzle locations. Ensures the level supports the intended gameplay sequence and replaces or adjusts placeholder geometry as development progresses.

Gilad - Player and Core Game Systems
Responsible for player movement, camera controls, interaction mechanics, and the central game-management systems. Manages game states, objectives, timers, resets, and shared events that allow the puzzles, guard, CCTV, cell, and other systems to communicate.

Daniel - Guard Behavior
Responsible for creating and implementing the guard character and its preset behavior. Handles animations, waypoint movement, idle behavior, the distraction response, collision, and integration with the puzzle and restart systems.

Blake - Story, Global UI, Audio, and Presentation
Responsible for the game's story and overall presentation, including objectives, menus, HUD elements, success/failure screens, global interaction prompts, audio, transitions, and visual polish. Also connects these presentation systems to game-state and objective events.

Brian - Puzzle Design, Programming, and UI
Responsible for designing and implementing all three puzzles, including their rules, clues, interfaces, timers, success/failure conditions, and reset behavior. Integrates the puzzles with each other and with the guard, CCTV, cell, objectives, and overall game progression.

Simeon - 3D Assets, Animation, and Visual Polish
Responsible for sourcing or creating final models, props, materials, animations, and visual effects. Replaces placeholder assets, ensures imported assets are properly scaled and configured, and provides final visual polish once the core gameplay is functional.
